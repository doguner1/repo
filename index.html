LongShot v1.1.1

com.doguner.longshot • v1.1.1 • Doguner

Smart scrolling screenshot stitcher for iOS. Automatically or manually captures vertically scrolling apps (Safari, X/Twitter, WhatsApp, Settings, Reddit, etc.) into a single, seamless, high-resolution image saved directly to your Photos library.

  • ✦ Auto & Manual Modes: Hands-free scrolling stitch or step-by-step custom slice capture.
  • ✦ Live Stop Control: Tap 'Stop' anytime to cleanly crop and stitch slices captured up to that point.
  • ✦ Hide Stock Thumbnail: Cleanly suppresses Apple's bottom-left screenshot thumbnail during capture.
  • ✦ Quick Respring Button: One-tap SpringBoard restart from Settings menu and navigation bar.
  • ✦ Native Preferences Panel: Full diagnostic logs, bilingual support, and configurable preferences.

CarSplit BETA 6

com.doguner.carsplit • v1.0.0-beta6 • Doguner

Run any two bridged applications side-by-side simultaneously on your Apple CarPlay display. Features independent dual-touch control, one-tap clock layout switching, and native Settings integration.

  • ✦ True Multitasking: Both apps stay 100% active with zero freezing.
  • ✦ One-Tap Clock Switch: Tap CarPlay status bar clock to cycle split layouts.
  • ✦ Requires CarBridge: Works seamlessly on top of bridged apps (Havoc / carbridge.app).
  • ✦ 11 Native Languages: English, Turkish, Vietnamese, German, Spanish, and more.

📦 Step-by-Step Installation

  1. Tap Add to Sileo or Add to Zebra above (or paste https://doguner1.github.io/repo/ into your package manager sources).
  2. Wait for repo sources to refresh and select CarSplit or LongShot.
  3. Tap Get → Confirm to install. When Sileo finishes, tap Restart SpringBoard.
  4. Go to iPhone Settings:
    • For CarSplit: Ensure CarBridge is installed, enable CarSplit toggle in Settings, and tap the status bar clock on CarPlay to split!
    • For LongShot: Take any screenshot — the floating prompt will appear immediately!
