CarSplit (Apple CarPlay Dual-Screen)

by @doguner • CarPlay Multitasking

Run two independent applications simultaneously side-by-side on your CarPlay dashboard. Navigate on Google Maps or Yandex while streaming YouTube or Podcasts. Features dual touch screen input, 1-tap clock layout toggle, native Settings panel with 10 languages, and zero audio pauses.

📦 Step-by-Step Installation

  1. Tap Add to Sileo or Add to Zebra above (or paste https://doguner1.github.io/repo/ into your package manager sources).
  2. Wait for repo sources to refresh and search for CarSplit.
  3. Tap Get → Confirm to install. When Sileo completes, tap Restart SpringBoard.
  4. Go to iPhone Settings → CarSplit and toggle Enable CarSplit to ON (disabled by default for safety).
  5. Connect your iPhone to your car, launch your apps, and simply tap the status bar clock to cycle split layouts!
